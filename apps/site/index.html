How it works

Four steps. You decide at every one.

  1. 1

    Ask

    Describe the change in a chat, like you would to a colleague. Tandem asks only what it needs to know.

  2. 2

    Approve the spec

    A short spec with acceptance criteria and the tests to add. Nothing is built until you, and your PM if you like, approve it.

  3. 3

    Copilot builds, Tandem checks

    Copilot writes the code on its own branch. Tandem runs your lint, types, build and tests, sends failures back, then reviews the change against the spec.

  4. 4

    Review and merge

    One clean commit and a pull request with every criterion traced to its test. A person reviews and merges. Agents never do.

Security and privacy

Built for teams who read the fine print.

Hosted in the EU

Tandem's data and processing stay in Frankfurt, backups in the EU. GDPR by design, with a data processing agreement for your company.

Your Copilot, your terms

Copilot runs under your own GitHub account and plan. We never resell it, and never train on your code.

Guardrails in code

Agents can't push to your main branch, merge, deploy, reach the network or run migrations. Enforced by Tandem, not by a prompt.

Secrets stay secret

Tokens are encrypted per workspace and never reach the browser. Sign-ins renew themselves and can be revoked at any time.

Every step on record

A tamper-evident audit log of approvals, commits and pull requests, exportable for your auditors.

Your data, your call

Export everything or delete your account yourself, at any time. No tracking cookies, no ads, no analytics on you.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which Copilot plans work?

Any plan that may use Copilot from the CLI or SDK: Copilot Free, Pro and Pro+, and Copilot Business or Enterprise from your company, on github.com or on GitHub Enterprise Cloud (*.ghe.com). Your organisation may need to allow the Copilot CLI policy.

Does Tandem merge or deploy anything?

No. Tandem pushes only to its own task branch and opens a pull request. A person reviews and merges. Deploys stay with your existing pipeline.

What does it cost to run a task?

Copilot work counts against your Copilot plan. Tandem shows an estimate in AI credits before you start, and stops a task at the limits you set.

Where is my data processed?

Tandem's own storage and processing are in the EU. Copilot is processed by GitHub under your agreement with them; with GitHub Enterprise Cloud data residency in the EU, that stays in the EU too.

Can we keep our source code inside the company?

Yes. Install the Tandem runner on your own machines. Tasks then run there with your own credentials, and the code never reaches Tandem's cloud.

Can Tandem run on my own computer only?

Yes. The local edition runs entirely on your machine, with the same workflow, for individual use.
